Our Story

Experience, Adventure & Affordability

In 2018, Heidi and Will took a trip that changed everything. They fell in love with the open road, the freedom of waking up somewhere new, and the simple joy of a home on wheels.

They decided to make that same experience available to everyone — not just those who can afford to buy. Color Wagon Rentals was born from the belief that adventure should be accessible, joyful, and a little bit colorful.

Based in Manitowoc, Wisconsin, we offer hand-painted, personality-filled campers ready for your next escape — whether you're heading to the Northwoods, Lake Michigan shore, or anywhere the road takes you.
