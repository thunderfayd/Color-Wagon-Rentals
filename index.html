Our Story

Experience, Adventure & Affordability

Color Wagon Rentals launched in 2026 with a simple idea: the freedom of a camper van shouldn't require buying one. Heidi and Will found two one-of-a-kind hand-painted Ford Econoline campers (cozy queen-bed interiors, rear-access kitchens and all) and put them on the road for everyone to enjoy.

Camper vans are the easy way into van life: they drive like a regular vehicle, park in a regular spot, and average around 16 mpg, the best gas mileage you'll find in an RV.

We're based in Manitowoc, Wisconsin, near the SS Badger ferry dock. Head for the Northwoods, follow the Lake Michigan shore, or hop off the ferry and into a van. Wherever you go, you won't blend in.
