How the Vans Are Laid Out

Both vans share the same smart layout: living space by day, queen bed by night, full kitchen out the back.

🛌

Convertible Queen Bed

The bench seat folds flat into a queen-size bed. Set it up for the night in under a minute.

🍽️

Convertible Daytime Table

Flip the layout for daytime — table up for meals, games, or rainy-day planning sessions.

🍳

Rear-Access Kitchen

Open the back doors to a pull-out propane stove, sink with 5-gallon water tank, and refrigerator.

🧰

Storage, Day & Night

Dedicated storage that works in both layouts keeps gear stowed and the living space clear.

🌙

Privacy Curtains

Curtains all around for cozy, private nights wherever you park.
