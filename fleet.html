Gertrude
2008 Ford Econoline · Warm, Woodsy & Full of Charm

Gertrude wears a hand-painted autumn woodland mural that turns heads at every campsite. Inside, her bench seat folds out to a queen-size bed, and the rear doors open onto a full little kitchen — pull-out propane stove, sink with water tank, and a refrigerator. Pair your road trip playlist to her Bluetooth radio and go.

The base camp...
The Trailer
Family-Sized · Delivered & Set Up For You

Our largest option sleeps up to 6 and has a full bathroom with a shower — perfect for families and friend groups who want a true base camp. Unlike the vans, the trailer comes to you: we deliver it to your campsite, set it up, and break it down when you're done.

📦 Delivery only: we set up within 15 miles of Manitowoc or Crivitz, Wisconsin. We handle setup and breakdown so you can focus on the fun part.
