Rental Agreement Terms

Color Wagon Rentals: Rental Agreement Summary

1. Driver Requirements: Renter must be at least 25 years of age and hold a valid driver's license. Renter certifies that they have provided accurate license information.

2. Deposit, Fees & Payment: A deposit is required to confirm your reservation. Our team will contact you within 24 hours to arrange the deposit payment. A $50 cleaning & prep fee and applicable Wisconsin sales tax are added to every rental. The remaining balance is due 7 days before the beginning of your journey. The security deposit is refundable upon return of the vehicle in its original condition.

3. Mileage: Each rental includes 100 miles per day. Additional miles are charged at $0.45 per mile.

4. Fuel: Vehicle must be returned with the same fuel level as at the time of pickup. A refueling fee will apply if returned low.

5. Smoking: Smoking inside the vehicle is strictly prohibited. A cleaning fee will be assessed for violations.

6. Pets: Pets are not permitted inside the vehicle unless specifically approved in writing by Color Wagon Rentals prior to the rental period.

7. Damage: Renter is responsible for any damage to the vehicle during the rental period beyond normal wear and tear. Renter agrees to report any incidents immediately.

8. Cancellation: Cancellation policy will be provided at time of booking confirmation. Please contact us for details.

9. Off-Road Use: Vehicle may not be taken off paved roads or designated gravel campground roads without prior written approval.

10. Return Condition: Vehicle must be returned clean and in the same condition as received. Interior cleaning fee may apply for excessively dirty returns.

By submitting this booking request, you confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to these terms. A full rental agreement will be provided and must be signed prior to pickup.
